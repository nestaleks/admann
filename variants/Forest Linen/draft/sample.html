Timeline

  1. 1. Project Kick-off

    The journey begins with brainstorming sessions, requirement gathering, and aligning the team on the project’s goals. This stage sets the foundation for everything to follow.

  2. 2. Design Phase

    Wireframes, mockups, and prototypes are created to visualise the user experience before moving into development.

  3. 3. Development

    The core coding stage where designs come to life. Features are built, tested, and integrated into the project structure.

  4. 4. Testing

    Rigorous QA ensures bugs are caught early. Performance, usability, and security checks take place before launch.

  5. 5. Launch

    The project goes live! Marketing campaigns, deployment, and real-world usage kick off this exciting stage.

  6. 6. Growth & Future

    Post-launch improvements, scaling, and continuous updates keep the project evolving and relevant.
